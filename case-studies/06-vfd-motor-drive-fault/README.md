## 3. Variable-Frequency Drive Operating Principles

A variable-frequency drive controls an applicable AC motor by regulating its electrical output according to the drive architecture and configured control method.

A common AC drive architecture includes:

| Component | General Function |
|---|---|
| Rectifier | Converts incoming AC electrical power into DC |
| DC link | Provides an intermediate DC energy stage |
| Inverter | Produces controlled AC output for the motor |
| Control electronics | Processes applicable commands, feedback, and control functions |
| Protective functions | Respond to applicable monitored operating conditions |
| Cooling system | Supports thermal management of drive components |

Drive architectures and control methods vary by manufacturer and application.

A drive fault indication may represent intended protective behavior rather than an internal drive hardware failure.

A reported fault indication alone does not establish the underlying cause.
