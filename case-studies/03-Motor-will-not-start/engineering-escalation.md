## 1. Purpose of Escalation

Request a qualified technical review of a reported motor
no-start condition affecting an industrial hydraulic
power unit.

The escalation is intended to:

- Summarize the reported operating problem.
- Present available information with source classifications.
- Identify technical uncertainties and evidence gaps.
- Organize possible fault categories without asserting a cause.
- Request appropriate engineering guidance.
- Define safe boundaries for further information gathering.
- Establish the documentation required for a technical disposition.

The current evidence is insufficient to identify a failed
component or recommend a corrective action.

## 2. Reported Equipment Problem

The fictional customer reports that the electric motor
driving an industrial hydraulic power unit does not run
after the normal start command is issued.

The customer also reports that:

- The equipment operated during the previous shift.
- A control-panel indicator appears illuminated.
- No unusual sound was reported during the attempted start.
- No alarm text or fault code has been supplied.

These statements have not been independently verified.

The precise behavior of the start sequence is unknown.

defined to isolate a failed component.

establish that all required power and control
conditions are satisfied.

does not establish that no fault exists.

No available evidence confirms an electrical,
control-system, motor, or mechanical failure.

**Assessment conclusion:** Insufficient evidence
for a component-specific diagnosis.
