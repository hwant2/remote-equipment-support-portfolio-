**Subject:** Technical Support Follow-Up — Intermittent Proximity Sensor Detection | SIM-SENSOR-004

Dear Customer,

Thank you for providing the initial information regarding the intermittent position detection condition affecting your automated equipment.

Based on the reported symptoms, we understand that the machine occasionally experiences inconsistent sensor indication and interruptions to its normal operating sequence.

The information currently available does not establish whether the condition originates from the proximity sensor, mechanical target positioning, electrical signal transmission, environmental conditions, or PLC input and sequence processing.

To support a more focused technical assessment, we would appreciate the following existing information, where available through approved records and authorized personnel.

### Equipment Identification

- Equipment manufacturer, model, and asset identification.
- Proximity sensor manufacturer and model.
- PLC manufacturer and applicable input module identification.

### Technical Documentation

- Applicable electrical and control-system drawings.
- Manufacturer documentation for the installed proximity sensor.
- Available mechanical mounting and detection target documentation.

### Operating History

- Description of when the intermittent condition occurs.
- Existing alarm and event records associated with the reported interruption.
- Available records describing sensor indication and PLC input behavior.
- Relevant maintenance, adjustment, or component replacement history.
- Existing records of applicable environmental and operating conditions.

### Existing Technical Findings

- Previously documented findings from appropriately qualified and authorized personnel, if available.

The requested information will help determine whether the reported condition requires further instrumentation, electrical, mechanical, or controls-related technical review.

At this stage, the available information does not support a recommendation to replace the proximity sensor or modify the control system.

Please ensure that any equipment inspection, testing, adjustment, or maintenance activity is performed only by appropriately qualified and authorized personnel following manufacturer documentation and applicable site safety procedures.

Once the relevant information is available, the proposed next step would be to assess evidence completeness and determine the appropriate scope of qualified technical review.

Thank you for your assistance.

Respectfully,
