## 5. Instrumentation Operating Principles

An inductive proximity sensor detects metallic objects within its applicable sensing range.

Detection performance may depend on manufacturer-specific characteristics, including:

- Target material and dimensions.
- Sensing distance.
- Mounting configuration.
- Supply and output characteristics.
- Switching response.
- Environmental operating limits.

These principles provide technical context only. They do not establish the condition or configuration of the fictional sensor.

## 9. Sensor Indication and PLC Feedback Limitations

A reported sensor indicator state does not independently establish:

- Actual target position.
- Correct sensing distance.
- Valid electrical output.
- Signal integrity at the PLC input.
- Correct PLC input interpretation.
- Satisfaction of machine sequence requirements.
- Sensor failure or correct sensor operation.

Likewise, a reported machine sequence interruption does not establish that the proximity sensor caused the interruption.
