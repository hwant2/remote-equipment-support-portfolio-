Dear Customer,

Thank you for providing the initial information regarding the intermittent positioning inconsistency affecting your automated robotic production cell.

Based on the reported symptoms, we understand that the robot begins its automated production sequence normally but occasionally exhibits an apparent deviation between the intended tooling position and the expected workpiece location.

We also understand that some production cycles reportedly complete without an apparent positioning deviation.

At this stage, the available information does not establish the magnitude, direction, frequency, or operating conditions associated with the reported positioning inconsistency.

The reported deviation does not necessarily indicate a defective robot controller, servo motor, encoder, or mechanical component.

The condition may involve the relationship between robot-axis feedback, mechanical movement, coordinate references, tooling configuration, workpiece positioning, operating conditions, or applicable control-system commands.

It is also important to distinguish positioning accuracy from repeatability. A robot may consistently achieve a position that differs from the intended reference, while another positioning condition may involve variation between repeated production cycles.

Additional documented information is necessary to distinguish these possibilities.

To support a more focused technical assessment, we would appreciate the following existing information, where available through approved documentation and authorized personnel.

### Robot and Controller Identification

- Robot manufacturer, model, asset identification, and axis configuration.
- Robot controller model, software revision, and applicable documentation.
- Applicable electrical, mechanical, and control-system drawings.

### Control-System and Operating Information

- Existing PLC command, feedback, and industrial communication records.

- Description of the circumstances associated with the reported positioning inconsistency.

Where existing records include timestamps, production cycle identifiers, coordinate references, measurement units, or applicable configuration information, preserving those details may support further qualified technical assessment.
