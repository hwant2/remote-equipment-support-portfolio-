# Supporting Evidence: Hydraulic Pressure Loss

[Back to case overview](README.md)

All information below is synthetic and created for this exercise.

## 1. Customer Intake Questions

### Equipment

- What are the model and serial number?
- Is the hydraulic circuit drawing available?
- What manufacturer instructions apply?

### Operating Conditions

- What operation is being attempted?
- Has the load or application changed?
- Does the issue affect one movement or several?
- Does the issue occur immediately or after warming up?

### Maintenance History

- What maintenance was recently completed?
- Were components replaced or settings changed?
- Was normal operation verified afterward?

### Available Evidence

- Are there alarms or maintenance records?
- Are recorded pressure and temperature observations available?
- Who collected the observations, and under what conditions?
- Is an authorized technician available?

## 2. Synthetic Observations

| Observation | Source | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Cylinder moves unloaded | Customer report | Loaded performance is not established |
| Cylinder stalls under usual load | Customer report | Load has not been independently verified |
| Motor appears to keep running | Customer report | Electrical performance has not been measured |
| No external leakage is visible | Customer report | Internal leakage is not ruled out |
| Symptom followed maintenance | Fictional maintenance note | Timing does not establish cause |
| Pressure behavior differs from normal | Technician report | Instrument and test conditions are unverified |

## 3. Missing Information

- Hydraulic circuit drawing
- Component specifications
- Details of recent maintenance
- Verified operating load
- Instrument verification
- Manufacturer-approved diagnostic results

## 4. Evidence Assessment

No single observation identifies the cause.

The maintenance timing is relevant but does not prove that
maintenance caused the problem.

Further testing must be selected using the correct equipment
documentation and performed by authorized personnel.

## 5. Next Evidence Request

Obtain:

1. Equipment documentation
2. Maintenance work order
3. Confirmation of installed component identification
4. Instrument verification
5. Approved test results and operating conditions
